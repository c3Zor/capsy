How it works

Drop. Fill. Release.

Three moves, thirty seconds a day. The point isn't tracking your stress — it's noticing it, and letting it go on purpose.

Drop

Log the moment something lands on you — light, medium or heavy. A voxel pebble falls into your creature with a satisfying plop.

Fill

Capsy fills up through the day. You see it on your Lock Screen. His face tells you how it's going before you've even thought about it.

Release

Four breaths, led by him. In through the nose, out through the mouth — and the stress literally evaporates, cube by cube.
