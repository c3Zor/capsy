Privacy Policy

Effective: 20 July 2026 · App: Capsy: Stress Relief Pet (iOS)

The short version: Capsy has no accounts, no analytics, no trackers and no servers of its own. Everything you log stays on your device. We couldn't read your data even if we wanted to — there is nowhere it gets sent.

What Capsy stores

Stress drops, breathing-ritual history, habits, game progress (gold, XP, streaks, unlocked rewards) and preferences (vessel, theme, sound). All of it is stored locally on your device in the app's private database, and shared only with Capsy's own widgets on the same device via Apple's App Group container.

What Capsy never collects

  • No account, email, name or any personal identifier — there is no sign-up.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. No third-party code at all.
  • No server-side storage — we operate no backend for user data.
  • No sale or sharing of data with anyone, because we hold none.

Apple Health

With your explicit permission, Capsy writes your completed breathing rituals to Apple Health as mindful minutes, and reads your heart-rate variability (HRV) to show a quiet body signal on the home screen. Health data is read on-device, shown to you, and never stored by Capsy, never transmitted anywhere, and never used for any other purpose. You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Health.

Purchases

Capsy Plus subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your payment details. Purchase entitlements are verified on-device via Apple's StoreKit.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Capsy schedules gentle local reminders (morning and evening). These are generated on your device — no push servers are involved.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and all Capsy data is gone. That's it — there is no copy anywhere else. (Mindful-minute entries you chose to save to Apple Health remain in Health until you remove them there, since Apple Health belongs to you, not to Capsy.)

Children

Capsy is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if we add optional cloud backup), the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date before it applies.
